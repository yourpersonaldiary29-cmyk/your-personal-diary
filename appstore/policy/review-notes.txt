Your Personal Diary - notes for App Review

WHAT IT IS
A private diary: entries with optional mood, energy, stress, sleep and activity fields, habits with reminders, a calendar, search, statistics and insights about the user's own entries, in 20 languages. Free: no purchases, no subscriptions, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no third-party SDKs.

SIGN-IN
The diary is kept in the user's account on our own server (https://yourpersonaldiary.fly.dev), so an account is required; the same diary appears on every device the user signs in on. Only our own accounts exist (user name or e-mail + password; no third-party login). Please tap "Log in" and use the demo account: user name "stor", with the password given in the Password field of App Review Information. No second factor, no e-mail code. The demo diary already contains sample entries and habits. Please do not delete this account or change its password; to test account deletion, create an account of your own with "Sign up" and delete it.

WHERE TO FIND THINGS
- Delete account: Settings > Delete account (the last card). It asks for the password and immediately deletes the account, its diary, habits and settings from the server. For people who cannot sign in: https://yourpersonaldiary.fly.dev/delete-account/
- Privacy Policy: Settings > Privacy > "Privacy policy", and on the Sign up screen. https://yourpersonaldiary.fly.dev/privacy/
- Terms of Use: Settings > Privacy > "Terms of Use", and on the Sign up screen, where one checkbox confirms both documents. https://yourpersonaldiary.fly.dev/terms/
- Offline mode: sign in with "Remember me" on, open a few pages, then turn on Airplane Mode. A bar says there is no connection; new entries are saved on the device, encrypted (the key is kept in the Keychain), and uploaded automatically when the connection returns.
- Face ID / Touch ID: while offline, the lock screen offers "Unlock with this device's screen lock" (Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode); typing the password also works offline. Lock at any time: Settings > Security > "Lock now".
- Reminders: set a time per habit on the Habits page and turn reminders on in Settings > Reminders (notifications scheduled on the device).
- Backup, restore and export (TXT, CSV, JSON, PDF): Settings > Backup.
- Language: Settings > Language.

WHY IT IS MORE THAN A WEB SITE (Guideline 4.2)
The whole interface is packaged in the app; no code is downloaded at run time. Reading and writing work without a connection, from an encrypted copy on the device whose key is protected by the Keychain. Native Face ID / Touch ID unlock, native local notifications for habit reminders, native file export and backup, and "Open with" for backup files. 20 languages including right-to-left Hebrew and Arabic, and screen-reader (VoiceOver) support.

DATA
Search, statistics and insights are computed on our own server from the user's own entries. No third party and no AI service receives diary data. Search questions are not stored. The diary file on the server is encrypted with a key derived from the user's password.

HEALTH
The optional sleep, stress and activity fields are the user's own notes. The app offers no medical service, diagnosis or advice; the insights only describe possible relationships in the user's own entries and say so in the app.

MAC APP
The Mac app is the same diary and the same account; please sign in with the same demo account. Offline, the lock screen offers Touch ID or the Mac password; reminders appear while the app is open. The Mac App Store build runs in the App Sandbox with outgoing network connections and user-selected files only.
