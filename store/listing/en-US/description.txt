Your Personal Diary is a private, smart diary that keeps your diary in your own account on the Your Personal Diary server. To use it you create an account in the app, and you need an internet connection to create it and to sign in for the first time. You write about your day, and the app helps you notice patterns over weeks, months and years. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Write your day
- Entries in your own words, with mood, emotions, energy, stress, sleep, physical activity, events, people, place, notes and tags.
- A timeline of your diary and a page for every day.

Habits and reminders
- Daily and weekly habits that you tick off as you go.
- Optional reminders, shown as Windows notifications while the app is running.

Gentle insights
- The app looks for possible relationships in your own data, for example between sleep and mood, or between physical activity and energy.
- Insights are worded carefully and never claim that one thing causes another.
- "Why did I get this insight?" shows how many days were checked and the exact numbers behind each insight.
- When there is not enough data, the app says so instead of guessing.

Calendar, statistics and search
- A month calendar in the Gregorian, Hebrew or Hijri calendar, with the holidays of the calendar you choose.
- Charts of your mood, energy, stress, sleep and habits over time, each with a data table.
- Smart search: ask a question in your own language, such as "On which days did I sleep less than five hours?", and get the answer from your diary.

One account on all your devices
- Your diary is kept in your own account on the Your Personal Diary server. Sign in with the same account on another device, and everything is there.
- What you change on one device appears on the others.

Keeps working when the connection drops
- Keep reading and writing. Your changes are kept encrypted on your PC and uploaded automatically when the connection returns, even if you closed the app in between.
- With "Remember me", the app also keeps an encrypted copy of what you viewed recently, so that it opens and shows it without a connection.
- Insights, search, statistics, backup, export and account settings need a connection.

Privacy and security
- Your diary is stored encrypted on the server (AES-256), in a separate file for each account. Your password itself is not stored.
- While you are signed in, the server opens your diary in its memory so that it can show, search and analyse it for you. The privacy policy explains what this means.
- The app connects only to the Your Personal Diary server, always over HTTPS.
- Automatic lock after a period of inactivity, and instant lock with Ctrl+L.
- Forgot your password? Reset it with a link or a code sent to your e-mail address, and keep your diary.
- Encrypted backup to a file that you keep wherever you choose, and restore from it.
- Export your diary to TXT, CSV, JSON or PDF.
- Delete your account and all of its data at any time in Settings.

20 languages
English, Hebrew, Arabic, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, Hindi, Thai, Spanish, Portuguese, French, Italian, German, Romanian, Greek, Polish, Czech, Hungarian, Russian, Bulgarian and Turkish. The app opens in English: choose your language in Settings, under Language, and your account keeps it. Hebrew and Arabic are shown right to left.

Accessibility
- Works with screen readers: clear headings, labelled fields and announced status messages.
- Everything can be done with the keyboard, with shortcuts for the main actions.
- Light, dark and high-contrast themes, adjustable text size and a reduce-motion option.

Please note
Your Personal Diary is a personal journaling tool for people aged 16 and over. It is not a medical or diagnostic tool, and its insights are not medical or psychological advice. If you are worried about your health, please talk to a qualified professional.
