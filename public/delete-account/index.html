Your Personal Diary - delete your account

This page is for the Your Personal Diary apps for Android and Windows and the website https://yourpersonaldiary.fly.dev, published by Ouriel Sebag. One account works in all of them, so deleting it once deletes it everywhere. You do not need to install the app to delete your account.

Delete it yourself (immediate)

  1. Open the Your Personal Diary app on Android or Windows, or go to https://yourpersonaldiary.fly.dev in any web browser.
  2. Sign in with your user name or e-mail address and your password.
  3. Open Settings and go to the last card, Delete account.
  4. Enter your password, tick that you understand it cannot be undone, and confirm.

The deletion needs a connection to the server. It cannot be undone.

If you cannot sign in

Write to yourpersonaldiary29@gmail.com from the e-mail address registered in your account, with the subject "Delete my account" and your user name. If you forgot your password, you can also reset it first with "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done. If the request does not come from the registered address, we will ask for confirmation from that address before deleting anything.

What is deleted

  • Immediately from the server: your account (name, user name, e-mail address), your diary with all entries, habits and settings, your sign-in on every device and any password reset requests.
  • The offline copy on the device where you delete the account. Changes still waiting on your other devices can no longer be uploaded and are lost; signing out on those devices deletes their copies.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Copies in the daily snapshots of the server's storage are deleted automatically within 30 days.
  • Copies in the operator's encrypted server archives (the server keeps the 2 newest, the operator at most the 3 newest offline) disappear as newer archives replace them, normally within three months.
  • Backup and export files that you saved yourself are not touched; delete them wherever you kept them.
  • Nothing else is kept. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account.

More details: https://yourpersonaldiary.fly.dev/privacy/
